Strengths Showdown

Scrum

  • Rapid feedback and adjustments
  • Encourages creativity
  • Higher team engagement
  • Early value delivery

PMI

  • Strong risk control
  • Predictable schedules and budgets
  • Clear accountability
  • Globally recognized standard

Weakness Face-off

Scrum

  • Less suited for profjects with fixed scope or regulatory requirements
  • Can cause scope creep without discipline
  • Requires high team commitment

PMI

  • Less adaptable to change
  • Can slow down innovation
  • Heavy on documentation and process
