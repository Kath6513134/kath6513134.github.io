1. Phase-Based Approach
    PMI methodology divides project into 5 parts:
    Concept and Initialization: Defining the project and its objectives. 
    Planning: Developing the project management plan. 
    Execution: Carrying out the work defined in the plan. 
    Performance and Monitoring: Tracking progress, managing changes, and ensuring the project stays on track. 
    Closing: Formally ending the project or phase.

Examples
    - Software Development:                 PMI methodologies are frequently used in software development to manage projects effectively, ensuring timely delivery and adherence to quality standards
    - Construction: In construction, PMI principles can be applied to manage complex projects, mitigate risks, and ensure projects stay on schedule and within budget.
    - HR Management: PMI methodologies can streamline HR processes like recruitment, training, and policy development, leading to improved efficiency and effectiveness. 
    - Operations: PMI can be applied to optimize manufacturing processes, supply chain operations, and IT infrastructure upgrades, leading to improved efficiency, cost reduction, and quality control. 
    - IT Infrastructure: IT firms can use PMI to manage upgrades, minimize disruptions, and ensure smooth transitions.
    - IT Projects: A recent IT project saw a 30% improvement in project success rates by implementing PMI methodology, with clear milestones and robust monitoring. Another IT project using PMI resulted in a 15% cost reduction through meticulous resource monitoring. 
    - Infrastructure Projects: The Gordie Howe International Bridge project, a major cross-border infrastructure build, utilizes PMI standards for its management, highlighting the methodology's applicability to large-scale infrastructure development.
    - Vodafone's Global LAN Project: Vodafone successfully used PMI standards to deliver its global GLAN project, achieving 100% site migration by the second attempt.
    - Sydney Opera House: While a challenging project with delays, the Sydney Opera House project offers lessons in managing large, complex projects under the PMI framework
    - Heathrow Airport Terminal 5: The construction of this terminal provides a successful case study of PRINCE2 methodology, a framework closely aligned with PMI, demonstrating its effectiveness in planning, execution, and delivery.
