Explore two of many project management methods and discover how each can lead to success

Scrum

An agile framework using sprints, roles, and artifacts for adaptive project delivery.

PMI

A structured methodology following PMBOK® standards to deliver predictable results.

Scrum Basics

Agile, fast-paced, and adaptive. Scrum focuses on short sprints, constant feedback, and teamwork to deliver results quickly.

PMI Foundations

Structured, documented, and process-driven. PMI follows a step-by-step approach using defined process groups and knowledge areas to ensure control and predictability.
